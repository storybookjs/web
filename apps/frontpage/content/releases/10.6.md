_New skills architecture for agentic workflows_ 

Storybook 10.6 contains hundreds of fixes and improvements:

- 💻 CLI bindings for agent tools/skills
- 🅰️ Angular-Vite MCP/skills support and improved docgen/snippets (experimental)
- 🟢 Vue MCP/skills support and improved docgen/snippets (experimental)
- 🧩 Tanstack / NextJS-Vite framework bugfixes
- ⚡ Improved performance and reduced bundle size

Browse the [changelogs](https://github.com/storybookjs/storybook/blob/next/CHANGELOG.md) for the full list of changes.

---

There are no breaking changes, but you can refer to our
[Migration guide](https://storybook.js.org/docs/releases/migration-guide) to upgrade from
pre-10.0 of Storybook.
